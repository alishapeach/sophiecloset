Guide
Click an image: copy its post code (Upstairs / Underground) or link.
Tags: click a tag to filter. All shows everything except Hidden.
Multi-select (Edit): tap images to select, then move, tag, or delete them.
+ Tag / × (Edit): add a tag, or delete one with its ×. Images stay under All. Hidden can’t be deleted.
Search: filter by name or tag.
⇅: cycle the order: Custom (your own, rearranged with ↑ ↓ in Edit) → Newest → Oldest.
↑ ↓ (Edit): select images, then move them earlier or later. Needs the order on Custom.
Rename: click an image, then hit Rename (or just type over the name). Search looks at these.
Stash: a second, separate shelf. Its images are kept, but carry no name tag.
Hidden: anything in this tag is kept out of All; open the tag to see it.
Share: upload an image for a quick shareable link, without adding it to the closet.
Backup: download every image on the shelf you’re on as one zip.
Edit: turn on editing to add or remove images.
Upload (Edit): add images to the shelf you’re on; hit Save to keep them.
Mouseover (Edit): the hover text carried in both post codes; hit Save to keep it.
Name (Edit): the name tag under the image in both post codes; hit Save to keep it.
Discard: undo unsaved changes.
Save: write your changes to the site. They stick, on every device.
